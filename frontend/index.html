How ToyBox Works

We are a platform only — pickup & drop-off is arranged directly between buyer and seller.

📦 ToyBox does NOT handle delivery. Once you find a toy, you contact the seller and coordinate pickup/drop yourself.

🕐 Renting a Toy

🎫
1
Get Membership
Pay ₹3,500 membership deposit. This is refundable and protects both parties.
🔍
2
Browse & Find
Search toys by category, age, city. View photos and details uploaded by sellers.
📞
3
Contact Seller
Message or call the seller directly through ToyBox. Agree on rental period and price.
🤝
4
Arrange Pickup
You pick up the toy from the seller's location or they drop it to you — mutually decided.
🧸
5
Play & Enjoy
Enjoy the toy for the rental duration. Take care — damages are deducted from deposit.
🔄
6
Return on Time
Return the toy as agreed. Not returning = blacklist. Return on time = all good!
