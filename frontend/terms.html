🧸 1. About ToyBox Platform

ToyBox is an online peer-to-peer marketplace that connects buyers and sellers of pre-loved toys across India. We are a platform only — we do not own, store, deliver, or handle any toys listed on ToyBox.

✅ ToyBox connects people. All physical transactions, pickup, and drop-off are arranged directly between buyers and sellers. ToyBox is never a party to these transactions.

By accessing or using ToyBox you agree to these Terms in full. If you do not agree, please stop using our platform.

👤 2. Eligibility

  • You must be at least 18 years of age to register.
  • You must provide a valid Indian mobile number and verify it via OTP.
  • All registration information must be accurate and truthful.
  • One person may hold one buyer account and one seller account only.
  • Blacklisted users are permanently ineligible to re-register without admin approval.

🎫 3. Membership & Security Deposit

3.1 What is the Membership Deposit?

Every buyer must pay a one-time refundable security deposit of ₹3,500 before accessing seller contact details or initiating any transaction.

⚠️ This is NOT a subscription fee. It is a security deposit held to protect sellers from damage, non-return, or fraudulent activity by buyers.

3.2 Deduction Rules

3.3 Deposit Refund

Your ₹3,500 is fully refunded when you close your account — provided no deductions, active rentals, or unresolved disputes exist. Refunds are processed within 7 working days.

🕐 4. Rental Rules

  • Rental duration and per-day price are agreed directly between buyer and seller.
  • ToyBox does not handle or process any payment between users.
  • The buyer is fully responsible for picking up and returning the toy by the agreed date.
  • The toy must be returned in the same condition it was received.
  • Late returns may result in additional charges determined by the seller.
🚫 Failure to return a toy results in permanent blacklisting and full forfeiture of the ₹3,500 deposit. Legal action may also be initiated.

🛍️ 5. Buying Rules

  • Buyers can purchase pre-loved toys directly from sellers.
  • Payment is made directly to the seller — ToyBox does not process payments.
  • Buyers should inspect the toy during pickup before completing the transaction.
  • If a wrong or misrepresented product is received, file a dispute within 24 hours of pickup.
  • ToyBox is not liable for the quality, condition, or accuracy of toy listings.

🏪 6. Seller Guidelines

  • Sellers must provide accurate descriptions, real photos, and correct condition grading.
  • Listings with misleading information will be removed and the seller may be suspended.
  • At least 1 real photo of the actual toy must be uploaded (no stock images).
  • Sellers are responsible for ensuring the toy is clean and safe before handover.
  • All new listings are reviewed by the admin team before going live.
  • Sellers who repeatedly receive damage or wrong-product complaints may be suspended.
⚠️ If a buyer receives a wrong product due to inaccurate listing, the deduction is applied from the seller's account, not the buyer's deposit.

🔴 7. Damage Policy

All damage reports must be filed within 24 hours of receiving or returning the toy. Late reports may not be accepted.

7.1 Filing a Report

  • Use the Damage Report Form.
  • Provide your transaction ID, at least 1 clear photo, and a detailed description.

7.2 Review Process

The admin team reviews all reports within 48 hours. Final deduction amount is decided after reviewing evidence.

🚫 Filing a false damage report results in permanent blacklisting and full deposit forfeiture.

🚫 8. Blacklist Policy

A user will be permanently blacklisted for:

  • Non-return of a rented toy after the agreed period.
  • False damage report with intent to defraud.
  • Repeated damage to toys across multiple rentals.
  • Fraudulent activity such as fake listings or impersonation.
  • Harassment or abuse of other users or ToyBox staff.

Blacklisted users cannot log in or create new accounts. Appeals can be submitted to support — reviewed within 7 working days.

⛔ 9. Prohibited Items

The following may NOT be listed on ToyBox:

  • Broken, unsafe, or recalled toys
  • Counterfeit or replica branded products
  • Items containing hazardous materials (lead paint, choking hazards, etc.)
  • Any item that is not a toy or children's educational product
  • Any illegal or restricted item under Indian law

🔒 10. Privacy Policy

10.1 Data We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, verified mobile number, city
  • Transaction data: rental history, purchases, deposit records
  • Usage data: searches, listing views, click patterns
  • Device data: IP address, browser type, device model

10.2 How We Use It

  • Authenticate identity and manage your account
  • Connect buyers and sellers on the platform
  • Send OTP and platform notifications via SMS/email
  • Process deposit transactions and deductions
  • Improve platform performance and user experience

10.3 Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data (name, city) with the other party in a transaction to facilitate pickup. Your mobile is only shared after a buyer request is accepted by a seller.

10.4 Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. JWT tokens manage sessions. All API traffic uses HTTPS. Data is retained for up to 3 years post account closure for legal compliance.

✅ You may request personal data deletion by contacting support. Requests are processed within 30 days.

⚖️ 11. Dispute Resolution

Either party may contact ToyBox support to raise a dispute. We review evidence and make a binding decision within 5 working days. ToyBox's decision on deposit deductions is final. For other disputes ToyBox acts only as a mediator and holds no legal liability for outcomes between users.
